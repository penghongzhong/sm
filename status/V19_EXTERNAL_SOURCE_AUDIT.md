# v19 external-source audit

## Plotnikov--Toland 2023

P. I. Plotnikov and J. F. Toland,
*Refinement of Helein's conjecture on boundedness of conformal factors when
n=3*, Annali di Matematica Pura ed Applicata 202 (2023), 1803--1833,
DOI 10.1007/s10231-023-01302-5.

Theorem 1.2 states, for
\(n\in W^{1,2}(D_1,S^2)\) with
\[
\int_{D_1}|\partial_1n\times\partial_2n|\le4\pi-\delta,
\]
the existence of an oriented orthonormal moving frame satisfying the
Coulomb-frame relations and a quantitative \(W^{1,2}\) bound with constant
\(c\delta^{-1}\|\nabla n\|_2\).

This is exactly the local moving-frame input used in
\`v19:prop:A-L2\`; the manuscript does not enlarge the theorem's hypothesis.

## Weinstein 1983

M. I. Weinstein,
*Nonlinear Schrodinger equations and sharp interpolation estimates*,
Comm. Math. Phys. 87 (1982/83), 567--576.

Used only for the sharp two-dimensional Gagliardo--Nirenberg constant and its
ground-state optimizer.  The Gaussian strictness argument and the numerical
deduction \(E_{\rm car}<4\pi\) remain internal to W20.

## Nahmod--Shatah--Vega--Zeng 2007

A. Nahmod, J. Shatah, L. Vega, C. Zeng,
*Schrodinger maps and their associated frame systems*,
IMRN 2007, rnm088.

This source is cited as corroboration for the Coulomb-frame formulation.
The load-bearing quantitative local frame estimate in v19 is
Plotnikov--Toland, while the global degree-zero patching/minimization is
spelled out in the manuscript.

## Keraani profile decomposition

Used in v15 and again in v19 for the inverse implication:
nonvanishing critical free Strichartz norm yields a nonzero \(L^2\)
Schrodinger profile after an appropriate symmetry extraction.
No nonlinear Schrödinger-map conclusion is imported from this theorem.

## Standard analysis retained explicitly

- 2D free dispersive estimate \(L^1\to L^\infty\);
- free \(L^2\to L^4_{t,x}\) Strichartz;
- Poincare on balls;
- pseudoconformal/Fourier formula for the free Schrödinger group;
- \(L^2\) Hodge projection;
- ordinary Fourier transform/density;
- constant-curvature Jacobi-field estimate on \(S^2\);
- finite and countable diagonal extraction.

These are the only non-paper analytic inputs in the v19 block.
